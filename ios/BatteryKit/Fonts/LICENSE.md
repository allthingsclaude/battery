# Bundled fonts

All three families are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, which
permits bundling in an application.

| Family | Weights | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Inter | Regular, SemiBold | https://github.com/rsms/inter |
| Space Grotesk | Bold | https://github.com/floriankarsten/space-grotesk |
| JetBrains Mono | Regular, SemiBold | https://github.com/JetBrains/JetBrainsMono |

They match the typography at https://allthingsclaude.cc — Space Grotesk for
headings, Inter for body, JetBrains Mono for technical and numeric text.
